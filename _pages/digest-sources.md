The digest covers the Chinese and world economy, technology and research. It draws on original reporting from authoritative Chinese outlets and first-party releases from statistical agencies, central banks, research institutions and developers.

Each edition links to the releases and reports used. Publication requires current evidence, a check against recently aired stories, and two separate MiniMax reviews of the Chinese and English scripts. An edition is held when the source material or checks are incomplete.
