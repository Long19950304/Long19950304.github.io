China Parcel Volume Exceeds 17 Billion for Two Months.
China News Service reports that the State Post Bureau released figures for October, when China’s express-delivery development index reached 477.8, up 0.5% year on year. Parcel volume exceeded 17 billion for the second consecutive month. The report estimated that October parcel volume would rise slightly year on year, while express-delivery revenue would increase by about 3%. Sub-indices also recorded year-on-year gains: the development scale index rose 1.2%, the service quality index 0.1%, and the development capacity index 2.5%. The report described integrated production, sales and transport services supporting seasonal food distribution. It also said companies expanded services around airports, railway stations, scenic areas, hotels and speciality districts during the National Day holiday. These included appointment collection, luggage delivery, packing, consignment and on-site counter services. The State Post Bureau expected service capacity to increase further in November to meet peak-season delivery demand and anticipated stable operation. These are reported figures and forecasts; the October revenue estimate and November outlook were projections rather than final observed results.

Canada’s October Home Sales Rise Slightly.
CBC Business, citing the Canadian Real Estate Association, reported that Canadian home sales rose 0.3 per cent month over month in October 2026. That still placed sales 10.6 per cent below October 2025 levels. CREA senior economist Shaun Cathcart described little activity between September and October, noting that the result differed from some analysts’ expectations. The average home price was $656,200, down 3.1 per cent from 12 months earlier. At the end of October, 197,000 homes were listed through Canadian MLS platforms, slightly above the long-term average. Cathcart said Ontario and British Columbia remained slow, while prairie and East Coast markets were hotter than many people might realize. He expected national sales to remain relatively flat in the coming months as buyers waited for more certainty about interest rates. These figures describe October 2026 activity; they do not establish why sales moved or predict a later housing result.

Panzhihua Water Plant Launches Integrated Microgrid.
China News Service reported on November 17, 2026, that Sichuan’s first integrated grid-forming microgrid entered operation at the Bingcaogang Water Plant in Panzhihua. The system combines excess water-pressure generation, a 1,000-kilowatt distributed photovoltaic installation, a small wind generator, energy storage, and the plant’s pumping load. Its design uses a 750-volt direct-current bus to coordinate these components locally and reduce repeated alternating-current and direct-current conversion losses. The report says storage follows supply and demand, storing excess renewable electricity and releasing it when generation is insufficient. The project can switch between grid-connected and islanded modes, supports black start, and has a reported supply reliability rate of 99.99%. China News Service also reported that the installation includes an electromagnetic transient simulation model and an energy-management system intended for participation in electricity-market activities. However, the source provides no independent verification of the performance figures, and the reported rollout to other facilities remains prospective rather than confirmed.

Deep-learning model rebuilds gap-free sea surface temperature at 10 km scale.
The Chinese Academy of Sciences Institute of Oceanology reports new progress on gap-free sea surface temperature reconstruction, using a deep-learning framework the team calls remote sensing cross-scale reconstruction, or RSCR. The problem it addresses is familiar from satellite work: infrared sea surface temperature imagery is detailed but often blocked by clouds, while microwave readings see through cloud cover at coarser resolution. The team combines both, drawing fine structure from VIIRS infrared data and broad background constraints from AMSR2 microwave data, alongside a continuous seven-day time series to represent how temperatures evolve. The published paper describes a network built from positional encoding, a time-series encoder, a difference encoder, an attention fusion module and a multimodal decoder. Reported results say RSCR outperforms the operational MUR product across regions and seasons, with higher structural similarity and lower error. Power spectral density analysis indicates the model retains physically meaningful small-scale variance at short wavelengths, supporting ten-kilometre gap-free reconstruction rather than mere grid smoothing. As a case study, the team applied the model to the 2023 typhoon Doksuri, reporting that the recovered cooling in the cold wake core is closer to VIIRS reference observations and consistent with Argo float measurements. The work appeared in the ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing, with support from national key research and development and natural science funding programmes. The source notes a limitation worth keeping in mind: evaluation depends on available reference observations, and cloud-free high-resolution truth remains difficult to obtain.

One-Atom Layer Reveals Interfacial Quantum State.
ScienceDaily, reporting materials supplied by the University of Osaka, describes a 2026 study published in Communications Materials. Researchers created a single atomic layer of ytterbium and copper on a copper crystal and used synchrotron light to examine electrons at the boundary. The reported measurements identified two distinct heavy-fermion states. One was concentrated mainly within the two-dimensional atomic layer, while the other extended into the three-dimensional copper below. The team reports that this second state provided direct evidence of heavy-fermion behavior generated through interactions across the interface. According to the account, localized ytterbium electrons hybridized with mobile conduction electrons in the copper substrate, causing electrons to behave as though unusually heavy. Senior author Shin-ichi Kimura said the next goal is to engineer and control such states and investigate quantum states including unconventional superconductivity. The suggested reading is that engineered material boundaries may become a platform for unusual electronic behavior. That application remains prospective, not demonstrated. The study also faces the documented limitation that ScienceDaily says its supplied content may be edited for style and length, so the full journal article is needed for a complete methods and limitations review.

GitHub’s Agent Host Protocol for portable AI sessions.
GitHub Engineering reports that its open Agent Host Protocol lets agent sessions continue across VS Code, the Copilot app, and cloud sandboxes. AHP organizes functionality around URI-identified channels, including required root, session, and chat channels. The protocol supports reconnection and replay, and libraries are available for Rust, TypeScript, Kotlin, Swift, .NET, and Go. GitHub describes implementation experience rather than an independent evaluation.

Ask Maps adds trip planning and appointment search.
Google’s November 16, 2026 product update describes a broader rollout of Ask Maps features for travel and local errands. Users can build day-by-day itineraries with numbered stops, adjust schedules, obtain directions, and share plans or export them to Google Docs on desktop. Google says route details can include live traffic, whether a place is open, weather, potential delays, tolls, parking, and vehicle charging information. New agentic features can search online and call local businesses for beauty, pet care, auto repair, and home repair appointments. Users can also search saved lists and check live availability for group plans. The publisher says updates are beginning on Android and iOS. Travel itineraries and list search are available wherever Ask Maps is offered. By contrast, route tips and appointment finding are launching first in the United States, with additional regions to follow. Google also says Ask Maps is expanding to 30 European countries, bringing its reach to more than 200 countries and territories. The company’s summary describes these capabilities as generally available, although the detailed rollout wording says updates are starting to roll out. For a reproducible check, compare feature visibility by device and region before drawing conclusions about practical performance.
