My research connects communication questions with **AI for Social Science**. I study how people use and trust media, how health and social issues are framed, and how AI can support reliable social science research.

## Health and Public Communication

I examine media use, health literacy, trust, and news framing through empirical studies of children and families, media audiences in Macau, and newspaper coverage of social issues.
