Unless explicitly stated otherwise, site content is provided for non-commercial research and teaching reference only. Commercial use requires prior written permission from the relevant rights holders. Any permission offered by this notice applies only to content I am entitled to license; it does not extend permissions for student work or other third-party materials or restrict uses permitted by law.

## Research and teaching
{: #research-teaching }

For content I am entitled to license, and which has no separate licence, consultation, appropriate quotation, and classroom display are permitted for non-commercial research and teaching. Credit the original author and work, and link to the original page. This permission excludes student work and other third-party materials, whose use is subject to the provisions below, any specific licence, and applicable law.

Public display does not grant unrestricted permission to download, republish, adapt, compile, or redistribute content. Obtain permission from the relevant rights holders for uses beyond the reference, quotation, and classroom display described above. Content must not be used in sales, paid courses, commercial advertising, brand promotion, or other products and services intended for profit without the required permission.

## Copyright and display of student work
{: #student-rights }

Students retain copyright in their original expression as provided by law; rights in third-party materials remain with the relevant rights holders. Submitting an assignment, agreeing to display, or choosing anonymity does not transfer copyright to the teacher or this site. Display on this site must remain within the author's agreed scope and does not confer exclusive rights or a right to sublicense the work.

Student work is displayed for viewing and learning reference. Display does not grant visitors a licence to copy or reuse it. Unless permitted by law or an explicit licence for the work, downloading and retaining copies, republication, adaptation, compilation, redistribution, model training, and commercial use require prior permission covering the proposed use from the author and any other relevant rights holders. Research, teaching, or another non-commercial purpose does not itself grant permission, and attribution does not replace permission. This site cannot approve uses that a student has not authorized.

When using work under the law or a licence, retain the author's credit, work title, source, and existing rights notices. Do not falsely claim authorship, plagiarize, or distort or alter the work in ways that infringe the author's rights. Credit anonymous contributions as “Anonymous student” or “Anonymous author,” as displayed, and do not infer or disclose their identity. Use of AI tools does not make a work freely reusable; protection and ownership depend on the original expression, third-party materials, and applicable rules.

Authors may contact [zhilongzhao@wyu.edu.cn](mailto:zhilongzhao@wyu.edu.cn) to correct their credit, switch to anonymous display, or request removal from this site. Include the work's page and information sufficient to verify authorship; there is no need to publish identity documents. Verified requests will be handled promptly in line with the author's wishes. Removing a work here cannot guarantee removal of copies saved or republished by third parties. This site can assist with enquiries about unauthorized use, but permission remains a decision for the relevant rights holders.

## Third-party materials and existing licences
{: #third-party }

Papers, news quotations, images, music, video excerpts, fonts, software, character designs, and other third-party materials remain subject to their original licences, publishing agreements, and applicable law. “Non-commercial research and teaching” is not a blanket permission for these materials. Check the source and terms for each item, and contact the relevant rights holders when permission is unclear.

Content with an explicit licence continues to follow that licence, including open-access papers, Creative Commons materials, and open-source code. The site theme and other open-source software retain their original licences. This notice does not revoke or change permissions already granted.

## Personal information and AI-assisted materials

Names, likenesses, voices, and other personal information must not be used for commercial promotion, identification, or impersonation without the necessary consent. Uses involving face replacement, voice cloning, digital replicas, or model training require a separate check of the necessary permissions; public availability does not itself grant them.

AI-assisted works may contain factual, textual, or visual errors. Verify their accuracy and any third-party rights before using them in research, teaching, or further production.

## Permissions and corrections
{: #permissions-contact }

For permission requests or concerns about copyright, likeness, privacy, or other rights, contact [zhilongzhao@wyu.edu.cn](mailto:zhilongzhao@wyu.edu.cn). Include the relevant page, the basis of your rights, and the requested action so that the material can be reviewed and, where appropriate, corrected, removed, or otherwise addressed.
