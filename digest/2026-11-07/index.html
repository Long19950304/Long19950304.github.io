The Newport-based company has grown into an international supplier of aircraft engines, parts and maintenance services, employing more than 230 people across sites in the UK and worldwide. AerFin buys, sells, leases and repairs aircraft, engines and components, and the company claims to serve more than 600 organisations. 3. ToyBlocks Offers Controlled Robot-Learning Tests

Hugging Face published a November 6, 2026 report introducing ToyBlocks, an open simulation framework for robot learning and evaluation. AllenAI reports reproducible experiments on data generation, model generalization, hierarchical planning, world models, and transfer from simulation to a physical robot.

Technology & research · Hugging Face · 2026-11-06 · Primary source

Read the full narration Hugging Face reported on November 6, 2026, that AllenAI introduced ToyBlocks, an open simulation framework for robot learning and evaluation. It combines a tabletop robot arm and blocks with automatic task and demonstration generation and behavioral metrics. According to the report, hundreds of task-and-motion plans can run in parallel on one graphics processing unit, reaching up to 24 times real-time performance. AllenAI’s initial spatial-generalization results for ManiFlow, π0.5 and MolmoAct 2 were poor despite large-scale pretraining, which the publisher presents as evidence that the problem remains unresolved. In another experiment, an agent using Claude Opus 5.5 solved or correctly declared tasks infeasible in 63% of cases; 50% of the scenes lacked required letters. The report says agent-written skill descriptions raised success from 24% to 63%, compared with 36% for a human-written description. For physical transfer, ManiFlow’s atomic-skill success was 80.0% in simulation and 81.1% in the real world. A hierarchical Molmo 2 plus ManiFlow system achieved 55.5% in 200 simulated episodes and 50.0% in 30 physical episodes. AllenAI said further trials are needed for statistical confidence.

Read the full narration Xinhua reported on 6 November 2026 that humanoid robots appeared in work-oriented settings at the ninth China International Import Expo in Shanghai, contrasting the displays with earlier shows involving greetings, dancing and boxing. Two robots played table tennis, while another adjusted its gestures and arms to take a reporter’s photograph with a Polaroid camera and hand over the picture. Other displayed tasks included acting as a hotel concierge, carrying luggage and preparing gongfu tea. The report documented unresolved constraints. Jilin University professor Ren Lei cited insufficient precise control, high motion energy consumption and interaction safety. Moze Lin said large models could direct robots to grasp large objects, but precision assembly still produced notable errors in the final few millimetres. Xinhua supplied no deployment volume, cost or commercial-outcome data, so the demonstrations do not establish scaled adoption.

5. Hugging Face Adds Egyptian and Nigerian English ASR Data

Hugging Face reports that a Liva AI collaboration adds private conversational speech datasets for Egyptian and Nigerian English to its Open ASR Leaderboard. The update also adds a Kachru Three Circles-based accent breakdown.

Technology & research · Hugging Face · 2026-11-06 · Primary source

Read the full narration Hugging Face announced on November 6, 2026, that its Open ASR Leaderboard would include two private Liva AI datasets covering Egyptian and Nigerian English. The Liva AI Conversational EG dataset contains 1.34 hours of conversational, spontaneous Egyptian English, transcribed with punctuation, capitalization, and disfluencies. The Liva AI Conversational NG dataset contains 1.55 hours of conversational, spontaneous Nigerian English, using the same transcription style. Hugging Face said the additions expand accent and language representation while supporting more trustworthy evaluations through private data and additional providers. It also introduced accent reporting based on Kachru’s Three Circles. The leaderboard now separates US from non-US English, including the United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, India, Nigeria, and Egypt. It also groups the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, and Canada as the Inner Circle, with India, Nigeria, and Egypt classified as Outer and Expanding Circles. Hugging Face said these broader groups will be divided further as more accents are added. The announcement did not disclose model rankings, comparative accuracy results, or the number of systems evaluated.
